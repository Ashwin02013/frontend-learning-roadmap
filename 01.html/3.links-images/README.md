- <a href="..."> makes a link, <img src="..." alt="..."> shows a picture
- Internal link = a page in your own site, External link = another website
- alt text is shown if the image fails to load and is read out by screen readers
- target="_blank" opens the link in a new tab



LINKS AND IMAGES = A CITY

│
├── <a href="about.html">      → A door inside your own building (internal)
├── <a href="https://...">     → A road to another city (external)
├── target="_blank"            → Opens the road in a new vehicle (new tab)
├── <img src="">               → A photo frame on the wall
├── alt=""                     → Caption under the photo for people who can't see it
└── <a><img></a>               → A photo that is also a door (clickable image)
